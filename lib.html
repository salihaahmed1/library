Welcome to Our Library

The School Library provides students and faculty with resources, books, and quiet spaces necessary to achieve academic goals and cultivate a lifelong love for reading.

Official Library Rules & Regulations

To preserve a productive and safe learning environment, all members must respect the following policies:

  • Silence Zone: Maintain strict silence inside the main reading halls and study cubicles.
  • ID Policy: A valid student ID or Library Card must be presented at the checkout counter to borrow materials.
  • Borrowing Limits: Students can borrow a maximum of 3 books simultaneously for a duration of 14 days.
  • Late Returns: A fine of $0.50 per day will accumulate for overdue library materials.
  • Care of Materials: Marking, highlighting, or damaging pages is strictly forbidden. Users must pay replacement costs for lost or ruined property.
  • Food & Drinks: Only spill-proof water bottles are permitted. Outside food or open beverage cups are not allowed near bookshelves or computing units.
